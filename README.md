# Dangerous_Dave_UI
Smoother Controls and Physics
•	You can control how high you jump by how long you hold the jump button.
•	You can steer your character slightly while in the air to land exactly where you want.
•	The game runs smoothly on modern browsers without glitching or running too fast.
Three Playable Characters
•	Classic Dave: Normal speed and a regular jump.
•	Recon Roxy: Faster moving and can double-jump in mid-air to reach high places.
•	Tank Bruno: Moves slower, but can walk safely right over spikes and lava without dying.
Built-In Tutorial
•	Level 1 has instructions written directly on the background wall to teach you how to move, avoid spikes, and open the exit door as you play.
Three Custom Levels
•	Level 1 (Training): An easy practice zone to learn the ropes and collect your first gems.
•	Level 2 (The Vault): A deep cave with harder jumps and traps you have to climb around.
•	Level 3 (Magma): A tricky lava level that requires very careful jumping.
Modern Visual Effects
•	Added "poofs" of dust when your character jumps or lands.
•	Gems and trophies burst into colorful sparks when you collect them.
•	The exit door turns from gray to bright green the moment you grab the golden trophy so you know it is unlocked.
